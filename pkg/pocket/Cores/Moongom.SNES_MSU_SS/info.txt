Super Nintendo Entertainment System (SNES), referred to as the Super Famicom (SFC) in Japan was the second home console produced by Nintendo.

This build (SNES_MSU_SS 0.8.5): R1 SNES core + MSU-1 (BGM and voice packs) + save states.
- Save states: plain carts, SA-1, Super FX (GSU), CX4, DSP-n, S-DD1 (NTSC and PAL),
  SPC7110 (experimental: saving while the game unpacks graphics can leave graphics broken after a load).
  No save states: BS-X, OBC1.
- MSU-1: the music track and its play position are part of the save state (resumes within ~0.4 s).
- Bitstreams (chosen automatically): main NTSC (plain, S-DD1, BS-X, FF6 voice, light gun), SPC7110,
  PAL (plain + CX4 + DSP-n), CX4/DSP-n NTSC, SA-1 NTSC/PAL, Super FX NTSC/PAL.
  The coprocessor bitstreams have no voice stream, no MSU-1 data track and no light gun.
- MSU-1: place game.msu (can be empty) and game.msp (+ .ms1 ...) or game-N.pcm next to the ROM.
  FF6 voice packs (NTSC main bitstream): use a ROM patched for the MSU-1 voice window ($2010-$2017).
