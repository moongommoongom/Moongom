Port by agg23. Core by srg320.

Super Nintendo Entertainment System (SNES), referred to as the Super Famicom (SFC) in Japan was the second home console produced by Nintendo. A 16-bit console that remains hugely popular to this day due to its expansive library and impressive enhancement chips.

Core supports 4 controllers (when connected to the Dock), and the SA-1, Super FX (GSU), DSP, CX4 and S-DD1 expansion chips. SPC7110 and BSX carts are NOT supported in this build. Please report all issues to agg23, as most likely any issues experienced are issues with the port, not the core.

This build (SNES_MSU_SS): experimental core with save states (all supported chips, including S-DD1) + MSU-1 (BGM .pcm/.msp and voice packs). Place game.msu (can be empty) and game-N.pcm / game.msp next to the ROM. Save states also store the MSU-1 music state (track, volume, repeat/play flags and position to about 1.5 s), so music resumes after loading. MSU-1 data/FMV streaming (Chrono Trigger movies) is not in this build.
Unofficial modified build, not supported by the original authors. GPLv3.

Save states: save with the Analogue button + Up; load from the Memories menu.
Super Scope / Justifier (light gun) support is not included in this build.
